About The Artist

LeafB

LeafB is a dynamic artist and creative visionary making waves in music, fashion, and visual arts. Born and raised in Alief, Texas, LeafB entered the music industry as one-half of the innovative duo Galaktiq. Together, they explored Fuji Pop — a fusion of EDM, R&B, Afrobeat, and Arabic influences. This marks the beginning of his solo musical journey, creating a platform to experiment and define his unique sound.
