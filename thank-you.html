About the Artist

Leaf B (Kalif Bakare) is a dynamic artist and creative visionary making waves in music, fashion, and visual arts. Born and raised in Alief, Texas, Kalif entered the music industry as one-half of the innovative duo Galaktiq. Together, they explored the genre Fuji Pop—a fusion of EDM, R&B, Afrobeat, and Arabic influences—and performed alongside top artists like Olamide, LAX, Idowest, and DJ Consequence. This marks the beginning of his solo musical journey, providing him with a platform to experiment and define his unique sound.
